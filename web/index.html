What you are looking at

Each session is a full order book rebuilt from market-by-order messages (every add, cancel, modify and trade, with order IDs), then sampled once a second. The chart shows the depth heatmap (resting size on every price, blue for bids and orange for offers, brighter is bigger), candles built from the tape, the session VWAP, large prints (seconds with unusually high traded volume, green when buyers were the aggressors), volume split by aggressor and cumulative delta.

Replay hides everything after the replay cursor: the chart, the depth of market and time & sales show only what was known at that second. Press Home then Space to replay from the open, or Shift-click the chart to start the replay at that time. While replaying, the Sim buttons trade with the same fill model as the backtester.

Backtests run in Python (ofbt/engine.py). A strategy sees one second at a time and cannot read ahead (tests/test_engine.py checks it). Each result is compared with two controls: the opposite side at the same seconds, and the same brackets at random seconds. A strategy only matters if it beats both on many sessions; the 90% interval is a bootstrap over whole sessions.

The sessions shipped with the demo are synthetic: a toy simulation, not market history. Sessions built from your own Databento files are stored under private_data/ and never leave your machine.
